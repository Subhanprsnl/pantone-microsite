About the event
For the first time, and for one day only, Pantone is opening its Color of the Year celebration to the public, and you’re invited.



Free and open to the public. Space is limited, so register now.



Step inside the story of the color and explore the cultural influences, creative movements, and shared emotions behind the selection. Through immersive installations, hands-on workshops, conversations, brand collaborations, and showcases, discover how one color can shape products, spaces, materials and experiences.



PROGRAMMING

Panel discussions are open to all registered attendees. 
Workshop participation is limited and requires a specific workshop ticket, which you can choose when you RSVP (available while supplies last).


12:30p – 1:15p · Fireside Chat: Why This Color?

Hear straight from the Pantone Color Institute on how the Pantone Color of the Year 2027 was chosen, and what it says about the world right now.



2:00p- 2:45p Color Palette Workshop: How Does Color Make You Feel?

What makes a color feel calming, energetic, nostalgic, or bold? Starting with the Pantone Color of the Year 2027, play with contrast, temperature, and proportion to see how one color can tell completely different stories. No design experience needed. You’ll leave with a palette of your own, built around one simple question: What do you want someone to feel?



4:30p- 5:15p Panel: Creating Your World Through Color

Color is how we show who we are, from what we wear to the spaces we live in. Join Pantone and brand partners for a conversation on how color goes from inspiration to the everyday things we love.



More surprises and partners will be announced closer to the event.
