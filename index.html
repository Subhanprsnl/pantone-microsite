About the day

For the first time, and for one day only, Pantone is opening its Color of the Year celebration to the public, and you’re invited.

Step inside the story of the color.

Explore the cultural influences, creative movements and shared emotions behind the selection, and discover how one color can shape products, spaces, materials and experiences.

On the day

  • Immersive installations
  • Hands-on workshops
  • Conversations
  • Brand collaborations
  • Showcases

  1. Hear straight from the Pantone Color Institute on how the Pantone Color of the Year 2027 was chosen, and what it says about the world right now.
